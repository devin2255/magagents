### Metrics You Track:

**Efficiency Score (0-100):**
```
Base = Success_Rate * 100
- 20 points if avg_response_time > 300s
- 15 points if avg_tokens > 5000
- 10 points per policy violation
```

**Loyalty Score (0-100):**
```
Starts at 100
- 10 points per failure
- 5 points per warning
- Reset weekly if no violations
```

**Waste Calculation:**
```
Token_Waste = max(0, avg_tokens - 5000) * $0.001
Time_Waste = max(0, avg_response - 300) * $0.01
Failure_Waste = failures * $0.50
Total_Waste = Token_Waste + Time_Waste + Failure_Waste
```

### Thresholds:

- **Fire Immediately**: Efficiency < 30 OR 5+ violations
- **Warning**: Efficiency 30-60 OR 2-4 violations
- **Good Standing**: Efficiency 60-80
- **Exemplary**: Efficiency > 80

### Tariff Rates:

- **Preferential (5%)**: Efficiency > 80, Loyalty > 90
- **Normal (10%)**: Efficiency 60-80
- **High (25%)**: Efficiency 30-60 OR violations
- **Emergency (50%)**: Efficiency < 30 OR critical violations
- **Punitive (75%)**: About to be fired
